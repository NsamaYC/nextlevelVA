Forehand on the Run – Video Analysis



Hip Rotation: Not turning his hips early enough. The right foot needs to pivot outward immediately to open the hips and accelerate toward the ball.

Elbow Position: The elbow is too close to his body. It needs to be higher and pointing outward to create better spacing and extension.

Legs: Needs to bend his knees and get underneath the ball instead of reaching with his upper body.

Mogul Step: At contact, needs to load and hit off the outside (right) leg, using the hips and legs to generate power while maintaining balance. Avoid crossing the legs too early.
