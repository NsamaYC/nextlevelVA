Backhand – Side View Video Analysis



Preparation: During the shoulder turn, the racket head needs to point upward. The right shoulder should rotate farther back to create better preparation.

Left Elbow: The left arm needs to be almost fully extended. Currently, the elbow is too bent, limiting extension and racket control.

Racket Drop & Legs: After preparing with the racket head up, drop the racket head below the ball and use the legs to generate power through contact.

Contact & Extension: After contact, extend through the ball and continue the follow-through with the left hand.

Racket Face: Avoid opening the right hand too early, which causes the racket face to open and reduces control.
