Forehand – Front View Video Analysis



Preparation: Elbow needs to be higher and away from the body, with the racket head pointing upward.

Shoulder Rotation: Needs to rotate the left shoulder farther back during preparation to create a better unit turn.

Racket Drop: The racket face needs to point downward during the drop before accelerating forward toward contact.

Extension: After contact, the elbow needs to extend much farther through the ball. Currently, he’s limiting his extension.

Follow-Through: Needs to relax the arm and allow the elbow to bend naturally during the finish. Currently, he keeps the arm too stiff and straight. The edge of the racket should finish by tapping the back of his shoulder.



Main Focus: Elbow up, racket head up, full left-shoulder rotation, racket face down, extend through contact, and finish with the racket edge touching the back of the shoulder.
