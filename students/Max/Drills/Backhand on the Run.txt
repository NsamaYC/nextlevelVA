Backhand on the Run – Video Analysis



Hip Rotation: Not turning his hips early enough. The left foot needs to pivot outward immediately to unlock the hips and allow him to accelerate toward the ball.

Footwork: Needs a quicker first step and better hip rotation to move efficiently instead of running with his hips closed.

Outside Leg (Mogul Step): At contact, he needs to load and hit off his outside (left) leg, not his inside leg. Currently, he’s crossing his legs, which affects his balance, stability, and recovery.



Main Focus: Early left-foot pivot, open the hips, accelerate to the ball, and execute the mogul step using the outside leg without crossing over.
