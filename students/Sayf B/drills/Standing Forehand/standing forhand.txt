Sayf – Standing Forehand Analysis



Shoulder Rotation: Rotate the shoulders more during preparation, creating a fuller turn without overrotating.

Elbow Position: Keep the elbow away from the body and in a straight line, not pointing too far down or too high.

Extension: After contact, fully extend the arm and elbow through the ball.

Follow-Through: Finish the swing with the edge of the racket touching the back of the shoulder.
