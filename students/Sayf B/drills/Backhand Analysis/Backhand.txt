Sayf – Backhand Analysis



Grip: Keep both palms firmly on the racket handle, not just gripping with the fingers.

Elbow Position: Keep the elbows slightly away from the body, with the right arm straight and the left elbow slightly bent.

Racket Position: Keep the edge of the racket pointing up without opening the racket face.

Extension: Extend both arms more after contact instead of cutting the swing short.

Rotation: Rotate through the foot and hip during the swing, keeping both feet grounded without jumping.
