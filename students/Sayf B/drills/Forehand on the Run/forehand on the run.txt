Sayf – Forehand on the Run Analysis



Hip Rotation: Unlock the hips by turning both feet in the direction of the ball. Run naturally instead of side shuffling with the feet facing the net.

Shoulder Turn: Improve the left shoulder turn to prepare earlier and create better rotation.

Legs: Bend the knees and get underneath the ball for better balance, power, and control.

Mogul Step: Use the mogul step when hitting on the run, pushing through the legs to maintain balance and recover quickly.
