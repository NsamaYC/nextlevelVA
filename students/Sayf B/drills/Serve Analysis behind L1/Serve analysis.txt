Sayf – Serve Analysis (Behind L1)



Ball Toss: Toss slightly more to the right and into the court. For a right-handed flat serve, aim around 12:30–1 o’clock.

Footwork: When bringing the feet together, keep the right foot almost in a straight line instead of crossing behind the left.

Body Position: Stay sideways longer during preparation. Rotate forward as you jump up into the serve, not before.

Jump: Use the legs more explosively to jump higher and reach a better contact point.

Pronation: Improve forearm pronation through and immediately after contact.

Landing: Land on the left foot while hooking the right leg behind for balance.
