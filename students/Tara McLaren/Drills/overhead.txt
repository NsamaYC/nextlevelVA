Overhead – Video Analysis



Footwork: Needs to start moving back with a crossover step instead of backpedaling. Turn sideways immediately and use crossover steps to get behind the ball.

Racket Preparation: She’s opening the racket face too early, with the strings pointing toward the sky. Needs to keep the racket up with the edge leading instead of opening the strings.

Elbow Position: Needs to keep the elbow bent and closer to the body during the initial preparation, like she’s getting ready to throw a ball.

Throwing Motion: Prepare with the racket pointing up and the racket face closed, then let the racket drop naturally before accelerating up toward the ball.

Contact & Extension: Reach up high, extend through contact, and finish the overhead with a complete follow-through.



Overall: Focus on early crossover steps, keeping the racket edge up during preparation, and developing a natural throwing motion instead of opening the racket face too early.
