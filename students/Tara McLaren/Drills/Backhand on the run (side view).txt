Backhand on the Run – Side View



Early Preparation: She’s waiting until the ball bounces to prepare. Needs to turn her shoulders and get the racket up immediately, before running toward the ball.

Shoulder Turn: Needs a full shoulder turn as soon as she recognizes the direction of the ball, then move with the racket prepared.

Racket Preparation: Racket is too low. Needs to keep the elbows more extended and the racket pointing up before dropping it into the swing.

Wrist Control: Too much wrist slapping instead of using her arms and body rotation. Needs to keep the wrists stable and extend through the ball.

Follow-Through: Needs to extend forward after contact and finish higher with a complete follow-through.
