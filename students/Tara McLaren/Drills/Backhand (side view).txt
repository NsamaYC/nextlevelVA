Backhand – Side View



Shoulder Turn: Needs to start the preparation by turning her shoulders more instead of using just her arms and wrists.

Racket Preparation: Racket is too low during preparation. Needs to keep the elbows more extended and the racket pointing up before starting the swing.

Racket Drop: After preparing with the racket up, needs to let the racket drop naturally before accelerating forward.

Wrist Control: Too much wrist movement and slapping at the ball. Needs to keep the wrists stable and use her arms, shoulders, and body rotation to generate power.

Extension & Follow-Through: Needs to extend both arms forward through contact instead of cutting the swing short. Finish higher with a complete follow-through.
