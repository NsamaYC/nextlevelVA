Forehand – Standing (Front View)



Leg Drive: Back leg is lifting too early. Needs to stay grounded longer and generate power from the legs instead of just swinging with the arm.

Hip & Foot Rotation: Needs to rotate with the back foot and hip after contact to transfer power into the ball.

Forward Extension: She’s going straight up instead of driving through the ball. Needs to extend forward more after contact.

Follow-Through: Racket is finishing too close to her ear. Needs to finish all the way across, with the edge of the racket tapping the back of her shoulder.

Forehand on the Run – Front View



Footwork: Taking too many small steps. Needs bigger, wider steps to cover the court faster and get into position.

Shoulder Turn: Shoulder rotation is too short. Needs a bigger shoulder turn to prepare the racket properly and generate more power.

Leg Drive: Not using her legs enough. Power needs to come from the ground up, using the legs and hips instead of just the arm.

Recovery: Needs to recover with a crossover step first, followed by bigger, wider shuffle steps to get back into position faster.

Footwork & Balance: Right foot is lifting too early while the left foot stays on the ground. Needs both feet grounded during preparation to generate power from the legs. Right foot should rotate instead of just dragging forward.

Racket Face: Racket face opens up toward the sky at contact. Needs to keep the racket face more closed and controlled through contact.

Forward Extension: Needs to extend through the ball, with the edge of the racket following the direction of the shot instead of opening the racket face upward.

Follow-Through: Needs to finish the swing completely, with the edge of the racket tapping the back of her shoulder.
