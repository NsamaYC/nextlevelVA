Backhand Video Analysis

Shoulder Turn: Rotate the shoulders earlier and more completely. Avoid preparing with the racket facing forward.

Racket Preparation: Keep the racket head up, with the strings facing sideways during the unit turn.

Footwork & Hips: Use the legs and hips to rotate through the shot instead of relying only on the arms.

Contact & Extension: Extend both arms forward through the contact point. Avoid cutting the swing short.

Follow-Through: Finish the racket completely over the shoulder with full body rotation.

Recovery: Recover quickly with a crossover step followed by adjustment shuffles.

Main Focus: Earlier preparation, better hip rotation, full extension, and a complete follow-through.
