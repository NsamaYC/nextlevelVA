Forehand on the Run – Video Analysis

Shoulder Turn: Rotate the shoulders more during preparation. The racket edge should point up, with the strings facing sideways instead of forward.

Hip Rotation: Turn the hips and outside foot earlier to run efficiently toward the ball.

Follow-Through: Stop pushing the ball halfway. Use the legs to generate power and finish the racket fully across the opposite shoulder.

Mogul Step: Use a proper mogul movement through contact to maintain balance and initiate recovery.

Recovery: Push off with the outside leg, crossover first, then use shuffle steps to recover into position.

Main Focus: Bigger shoulder turn, full follow-through, better hip rotation, and explosive recovery footwork.
