Sebastian – Forehand Video Analysis



Preparation: The elbow is dropping and bending too much. Needs to keep the elbow higher, with the arm almost fully extended (not locked) and the racket head pointing upward during the shoulder turn.

Shoulder Rotation: Needs a better shoulder turn while maintaining the elbow up and away from the body.

Hip & Foot Rotation: Not using his hips and feet enough to rotate through the shot. Needs to pivot with the foot and rotate the hips to generate more power.

Extension After Contact: Needs to extend the elbow and racket farther through the ball instead of shortening the swing.

Follow-Through: Currently stopping the racket in the middle. Needs to complete the full follow-through, keeping the elbow up and allowing the racket to wrap around until the edge of the racket taps the back of his shoulder.



Main Focus: Elbow up, racket head up, almost full arm extension during preparation, hip and foot rotation, extend through contact, and finish all the way around with the racket edge touching the back of the shoulder.
